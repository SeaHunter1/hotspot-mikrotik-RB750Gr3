Starlink Onboard Internet

Acceptable Use Policy

This onboard Starlink Internet. By connecting, every user agrees to use it responsibly and to follow the rules below so the network stays fast, safe and available for everyone on board.

What is not allowed

  • Torrents and peer-to-peer (P2P) traffic. BitTorrent, uTorrent, qBittorrent and any other P2P download or file-sharing client are prohibited.
  • Illegal downloading or streaming. Downloading or sharing pirated movies, TV shows, music, games, software or any other copyrighted material without permission.
  • Hacking and network abuse. Port scanning, intrusion attempts, spreading malware or viruses, denial-of-service attacks, or trying to access other users' devices.
  • Bypassing the network controls. Circumventing the captive portal, spoofing MAC addresses, sharing your login with others, or reselling or re-broadcasting the connection (e.g. as your own hotspot).
